# Code of Conduct

Bento is maintained for people who want safer personal and small-team storage. Project spaces should stay practical, respectful, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Discuss code, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than personal qualities.
- Respect privacy: do not post secrets, personal data, private logs, or private file paths.
- Use issues and PRs for reproducible problems, maintainable fixes, and clear questions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private data or credentials.
- Intentionally misleading bug reports, fabricated evidence, or unsafe reproduction steps.
- Pressuring maintainers to bypass security, validation, or review.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and may block participants who make the project unsafe or unproductive.

Report conduct concerns to kutaelee0@gmail.com.
